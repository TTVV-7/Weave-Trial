# Cults3D listing kit: Construction Sets 2 and 3

Ready-to-paste titles, descriptions and tags for the 26 set 2 and set 3 assets
(14–39 in [README.md](README.md)), plus a suggested free/paid split. Set 3 starts
at [Construction Set 3](#construction-set-3-2739). Prices are starting
points to check against similar listings, not market data.

## Free or paid

Mix the two rather than giving everything away:

| Tier | Assets | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | Jersey Barrier (single), Contractor Wheelbarrow, Wooden Cable Reel (and the set 1 Traffic Cone) | Small, quick to print, and useful in every diorama. They bring downloads, likes and followers, and they introduce the 1:50 scale. |
| **Low** (about $1–2) | Jersey Barrier Run, Type III Barricade, Portable Toilet, Pallet of CMU Blocks, Steel Beam Bundle | Props that fill out a scene. A buyer who already has the free barrier will want the run and the barricade. |
| **Mid** (about $3–4) | Shipping Container, Frame Scaffold Tower, LED Light Tower | Bigger centrepieces with more detail. |
| **Premium** (about $5–7) | Mini Excavator, Skid Steer Loader | The models people search for, and the most work. |
| **Bundle** (about 40% off the sum) | All 13, or all 26 with set 1 | Anyone building a whole site buys once. Link the bundle from every single listing. |

Keep every free listing at the same 1:50 scale as the paid ones and say so in
the description, so the free models lead to the paid ones. Post the
collection render (`Construction_Set_2_collection.png`) as the first image of
the bundle, and as the last image of each single listing ("part of a 13-piece
set").

**Leave brand names out.** Don't put manufacturer or company names in titles,
descriptions or tags. Use class names like "3.5 t mini excavator", "radial-lift
skid steer" or "20 ft ISO container". These models are generic, so you don't
need the names. The same goes for the two older models whose files carry
company or product names: retitle them before listing.

## Images for each listing

1. `<Name>_preview.png`: the coloured render.
2. `print_1-50/<Name>_1-50_preview.png`: what the print file looks like.
3. A photo of your own print, once you have one. It helps more than anything else.

## Files to upload for each listing

- `print_1-50/<Name>_1-50.stl`: the print file.
- `<Name>.step`: for people who want to edit, re-scale or split it.
- `<Name>.glb`: for people using it in Blender, games or renders. Make sure the
  licence you pick covers that use.
